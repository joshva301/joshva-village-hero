# Character art direction\n\n## Joshva (player)\n- Stylized-realistic adventure hero, not toy-like\n- Humanoid proportions, approx. 1.8 m tall\n- Practical village/adventure clothing, layered cloth and leather, modest armor accents\n- Clearly readable silhouette from an angled top-down camera\n- PBR textures: base color, normal, roughness; metallic map only where needed\n- Rigged; idle, walk, run, sword attack, bow aim/shoot animations\n\n## Enemy\n- Distinct silhouette and palette from Joshva\n- Approx. 1.85 m tall\n- Layered cloth/leather/armor with face and hair detail\n- Rigged; idle, walk, attack, hit reaction animations\n\n## Technical target\n- GLB/glTF 2.0\n- 1 unit = 1 metre; feet at ground level\n- Face +Z\n- Textures embedded\n- Prefer 10k–30k triangles per character for mobile; reduce if performance is poor\n- Keep animation names descriptive\n\nThe game loader looks for `models/joshva.glb` and `models/enemy.glb`.\n
